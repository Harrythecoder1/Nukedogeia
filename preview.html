    About NukeDogeia

    NukeDogeia is a remote island civilization that emerged after a catastrophic nuclear explosion at a forgotten power plant. Radiation reshaped both the land and its inhabitants. Over time, survivors formed a culture centered around Doge worship, believing these beings embody resilience, chaos, and guidance.

    Dwagons—mutated guardian creatures—roam the island, protecting settlements from external threats and unstable radiation zones. Society is organized through decentralized councils led by the Doge Priests.
